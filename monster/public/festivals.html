City by city

Portland, Las Vegas, Nashville, Chattanooga, Memphis, Orlando, New York, Seattle

Portland

Portland Horror Film Festival only (June 2027, call opens ~Dec). It wants a Portland premiere; nothing else in town is worth spending that on.
  • Portland Horror Film FestivalHollywood Theatre. 11 years, the genre fest in town. Shorts from ~$10 at the first tier. No US commercial/online play.
  • Portland PanoramaApr 2027. Dec 15 $40 / Jan 9 $50. Held — screens before PHFF.
  • Portland Film FestivalOct 2027. Mid-tier, closes Nov 18. Held.
  • H.P. Lovecraft Film FestivalOct 2027, 31 years, prestigious but cosmic-horror only. Only if the film fits; 2027 tiers run to Aug.

Las Vegas

Las Vegas FF (Dec 10, free) and Sin City Horror Fest (Jan 29, $18). Dam Short closed Oct 31 — next cycle opens Mar 2027 for Feb 2028.
  • Sin City Horror FestSep 2027. Jan 29 $18 / Apr 30 $20 / Jul 2 $25. Shorts ≤30 min, no premiere rule.
  • Las Vegas Film FestivalJan 14–21 2027 relaunch, fees waived. Dec 10 final.
  • Dam Short Film FestivalBoulder City, horror category, no premiere rule. 2028 cycle opens ~Mar 2027 ($35 extra-early-bird).
  • LV Int'l Film & ScreenwritingModest; 2027 call ~Dec. Low priority.

Nashville

Nashville Film Festival is off the list: its Graveyard Shorts need a Tennessee premiere and Chattanooga screens first. Nashville Horror can follow Chattanooga.
  • Nashville Horror Film FestivalOct 2027, 3 years, cheap ($14–29), no premiere rule. Call ~Feb.
  • Nashville Film FestivalSep 2027, Oscar-qualifying, TN premiere required. Skipped for Chattanooga.

Chattanooga

Chattanooga Film Festival, Dec 1, $65 — the Tennessee festival for this film. No premiere rule, so Knoxville Horror and Nashville Horror can follow.
  • Chattanooga Film FestivalJun 24–Jul 1 2027, in-person + virtual, 13 years. Shorts under 30 min. Zero tolerance on generative AI.
  • Nooga UndergroundOct 2027, scrappy local fest, opens Jan 1, $20–35 (TN discount).

Memphis

Indie Memphis, call ~May 2027 for Nov 2027. Requires a Memphis premiere, which nothing else on the list touches.
  • Indie Memphis28 years, Oscar-qualifying, top Southern regional fest. Shorts ≤25 min. Early ~May 1 $45 → late ~Jun 26 $65.

Florida Film Festival (Dec 9, $90) now; Freak Show at its early-bird (~late Jan); Spooky Empire in spring. Hold the Florida premiere for Enzian.

  • Freak Show Horror Film FestivalOrlando's horror-only fest, 21 years, Oct 2027. No premiere rule. Early-bird ~late Jan ~$25; ladder to $65.
  • Spooky Empire Film FestivalInside the horror convention, Oct 2027. 20 years, $10–30, no premiere rule. Call ~Mar–Jul.
  • Love Your ShortsSanford, Feb 2027, horror block. Closed Nov 1.

  • Coney Island Film FestivalApr 30–May 2 2027. Horror category, any runtime. Dec 18 $40 / Feb 12 $45.

Caveats

Everything was read off FilmFreeway on Sep 29, 2026. Festivals change tiers without notice. Where a festival's 2027 call hadn't opened yet (Portland Horror, Telluride, FilmQuest, Nightmares, Brooklyn Horror, Fantastic Fest, Screamfest, Beyond Fest, Grimmfest, Celluloid Screams, Sitges, Freak Show, Spooky Empire, Buried Alive, Terror in the Bay, Indie Memphis, NYC Horror, Nashville Horror, Knoxville), the fees shown are last cycle's and the timing is inferred from it — marked with "~".

Tribeca's 2027 page was still showing 2026 when checked; the Dec 3 / $70 figure is last year's Regular deadline. Currency conversions (€/£) are approximate.

Premiere sequencing: Florida Film Festival (April) and Popcorn Frights (August) both want a Florida premiere — enter both, give up Popcorn Frights if Enzian selects. Portland is held for Portland Horror. Chattanooga screens first in Tennessee. SIFF needs a Western Washington premiere; Fantasia a Montreal one; Fantastic Fest a Texas one; Tribeca no prior NY-region screening. Keep the film off public YouTube/Vimeo until the run is done — most of the top tier disfavors or bars films already online. A password-protected screener is fine everywhere.
